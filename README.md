## 1. Overview & Core Philosophy

**PixelCraft Pro Studio** is an advanced 83-in-1 image processing and creative editing suite built with a strict **Local-First, Zero-Cloud Architecture**. 

We firmly believe that your personal photos, documents, and creative designs should remain strictly yours. **Your photos never leave your device.**

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

1. **No Photo or File Uploads:** All compression, resizing, format conversion, background removal, filter applications, EXIF metadata extraction, and cropping execute entirely in your device's local CPU/GPU memory. Zero bytes of your photos are ever uploaded to any cloud server.
2. **No Accounts or Logins:** You do not need to register an account, sign in, or provide personal credentials to use any of the 83 tools.
3. **No Tracking or User Profiling:** We do not track your location, your editing habits, or the content of your images.
4. **No Third-Party Ad Networks:** We do not sell, rent, or monetize your usage data with third-party advertisers.

---

## 3. Local Storage & Device Permissions

PixelCraft Pro requests only the minimal system permissions necessary for image editing functionality:

- **Photo Library / Storage Access (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`):** Required only to let you select images from your gallery to process inside the studio.
- **Save to Storage (`WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / Document Tree):** Required only when you explicitly tap "Save" or "Export" to save the optimized or edited output image to your device storage.

All processing cache is stored in volatile memory (`RAM`) and is automatically cleared upon closing the tool or tapping "Clear Image Cache" in the Studio Hub.

---

## 4. Metadata & EXIF Privacy

PixelCraft Pro includes dedicated tools (such as **Tool #70: Remove EXIF Data**) specifically designed to sanitize private geolocation, camera serial numbers, and device timestamps before you share images on social media or messaging platforms.
