Official Privacy Policy • Effective Date: November 2, 2026

Zero-Cloud & Local-First Commitment

PixelCraft Pro never uploads, syncs, or stores your photos or metadata on cloud servers. All 83 tools execute 100% locally in your device memory.

1 Overview & Architecture

PixelCraft Pro Studio is a professional 83-in-1 image processing and creative suite. It was engineered from the ground up under a strict local-first architectural mandate. We believe your memories, documents, and personal designs belong strictly to you.

2 Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, monetize, analyze, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No Image Uploads: Every single pixel manipulation, compression, resizing, filter application, and format conversion happens completely on your device.
  • No User Registration: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide phone numbers, emails, or personal credentials to use all 83 tools.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: We do not log your device identifiers, IP address, browsing behavior, or photo contents.
  • No Third-Party Ad Brokers: We do not integrate data-harvesting advertisement SDKs.

3 Device Permissions

PixelCraft Pro asks for standard operating system permissions strictly when initiated by you:

  • Photo Library Access (Read): Used strictly to allow you to pick the photo you wish to edit in the studio.
  • Storage / Media Saving (Write): Used strictly to save the edited or compressed output photo to your device gallery or downloads folder when you tap "Save Image".

4 EXIF & Geolocation Privacy

Many digital photos contain sensitive embedded EXIF data, including GPS coordinates, camera serial numbers, and capture timestamps. PixelCraft Pro includes dedicated privacy tools (such as Tool #70: Remove EXIF Data) allowing you to inspect and strip sensitive metadata before sharing photos publicly.
